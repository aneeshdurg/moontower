title: 10,000 Days

---

Today, I am 10,000 Days old.

---

Today is my 10,000th Day on earth. This is a much nicer and more aesthetic
number than 27 years, 4 months, and 18 days. Arguably, this is far more
aesthetic and worth celebrating than my 27th birthday, but less people care. It
is somewhat disheartening to know that this is the last time I will ever live a
number of days that is a power of ten, but such is life.

Today, I can confidently say that I've done certain actions over 10,000 times.
Not particularly high-caliber ones, but I now KNOW that I've pooped at least
10,000 times, that I've slept at least 10,000 times, and that I've eaten at
least 10,000 times. Though the true count is higher for all of the above.

I can confidently say that compared to my 1,000th day, or even my 5,000th day, I
feel more like I've gained some mastery over the act of living. I would say that
my top 5 highlights of the last 10 kilo days have been (mostly
chronologically):

+ breathing
+ seeing
+ eating
+ walking
+ reading

My 10,000th day comes at a time when I've been thinking about this idea of how
we aren't aware of the subconscious skills we possess lately as I've been trying
to learn how to write fluently with my left hand (prompted by me fracturing my
right wrist), and the act of relearning something that has become so instinctual
to me has been an interesting journey. Every time I struggle to write with my
left hand I am reminded that countless hours have gone into creating the
distinctive font that my right hand emits.

I think the next aesthetic day count I'm looking forward to living through will
be my 11,111th day.
