title: How I learned to stop worrying and love brevity

---

LLM slop has taken over every facet of written media, but it's also made me feel
more okay pushing out less.

---

I'm tired of reading walls of AI-generated text that that uses many words to
say the most lukewarm of takes on linkedin. But it's also made me appreciate
concisely worded genuine statements from people around me. I realized that good
grammar, spelling, and formatting actually doesn't matter that much if you're
just direct.

Realizing this makes me feel like I don't need to try as hard to fill up my blog
posts with words. There's a lot of noise out there, but at my readers can know
that least every bit of the noise on this blog was written by me.
